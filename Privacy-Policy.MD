# Privacy Policy

# Reuben Hu (as a Developer)

I, Reuben Hu, don't collect, store, or process personal information from users. Your data stays on your device and is not shared with any third parties. (Unless you're sharing it.)

This means, **no telemetry is used in this plugin, in any way.** All crashes, bugs or issues are reported by you, the user, through GitHub Issues. [Link Here.](https://github.com/BenjaminFosters/CommandBeep/issues)

# Beeper Messenger

Beeper, owned by Automattic, has its own privacy policy, which you can find here: [https://beeper.com/privacy](https://beeper.com/privacy). Please read it carefully to understand how your data is handled by Beeper.

# Data Transportation Mechanism

Personal data in question are:

- Beeper Contact Name
- Beeper Contact Identifier (such as phone number or tags)
- Beeper Access Token (API key)

Between CommandBeep and Beeper, the data is transported through the Beeper API, either by default endpoint (`http://127.0.0.1:23373`) or through specified endpoint.

# Privacy Heads-up

Whilst we don't collect personal information, you might able to expose your data by accident or intentionally. Please before submitting issues, redact any sensitive information, such as contact name, associated identifier, or Beeper Access Token (API key).
